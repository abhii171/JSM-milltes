Our Heritage & Vision

Revitalizing Andhra & Rayalaseema's Ancient Millet Roots

Founded by Sowjanya and Manoj, JSM was created with a clear mission: to make everyday South Indian breakfast deeply nutritious, delicious, and accessible without refined flours, artificial colors, or compromises.

Growing up on rustic, wholesome meals of Ragi Sangati, slow-cooked Jowar Rotis, and stone-pounded grain tiffins, they noticed Hyderabad’s bustling mornings were dominated by polished white rice and maida. JSM brings back centuries-old wisdom straight to your plate.

Every dish is prepared using slow culinary methods, served clean on fresh cut banana leaves, drizzled with fragrant farm desi ghee, and complemented by traditional freshly grounded coconut, peanut, and allam (ginger) chutneys.
