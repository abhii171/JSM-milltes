## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, nutrition, and rooted culinary tradition of authentic South Indian millet and ragi tiffins. It merges ancient grain nourishment with modern gastronomic pride, designed for urban food lovers, health-conscious families, and traditional breakfast patrons across Hyderabad. 

The aesthetic is an artisanal synthesis of **Minimalism** and **Tactile Organic Warmth**:
- Rich, earthy forest greens anchor the brand in the vitality of plant-based culinary heritage and fresh banana-leaf service.
- Lustrous golden turmeric tones evoke clarified desi ghee, hand-pounded podi spices, and glowing brass tiffin vessels.
- Crisp cream surfaces paired with generous breathing room present rustic staples—such as steaming ragi idli, golden crisp dosas, and hearty ragimuddha—with elevated editorial respect.
- Interfaces avoid transactional clutter in favor of an inviting, sensory-rich culinary catalog that emphasizes community, warmth, and dietary transparency.

## Colors

The palette balances deeply saturated botanical heritage with appetitive culinary warmth.

- **Primary (`#0F3D2E`)**: Forest Evergreen. Conveys fertile agricultural heritage, fresh plantain leaves, and calm traditional hospitality. Used for primary navigation, deep section backdrops, high-emphasis headers, and structural badges.
- **Secondary (`#D4A017`)**: Warm Turmeric Gold. Inspired by pure melted ghee, roasted lentils, and brass service vessels. Serves as the primary indicator for currency symbols, highlights, active toggles, and delicate structural borders.
- **Tertiary (`#E5A93C`)**: Radiant Amber. A brighter golden touch applied to primary interactive callouts, star ratings, active states, and alert accents.
- **Neutral Surface (`#FAF6EC`)**: Warm Millet Cream. A tender, unbleached grain base that replaces harsh stark whites, minimizing eye strain while conveying a soft morning daylight atmosphere.
- **Pure Canvas (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean Plate White. Utilized for floating dish cards, dietary badges, and elevated surface tiers to mirror clean stainless steel and porcelain tableware.
- **Typography Dark (`#2B2B2B`)**: Smoked Charcoal. A high-contrast, softened black ensuring effortless legibility without the starkness of pure black.

## Typography

The type scale marries classical editorial majesty with contemporary legibility.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display)**: Infuses heritage, prestige, and timeless Indian hospitality. Reserved for hero mantras, category titles (e.g., *Ragi Minapa Dosa*, *Guntaponganalu*), and story-driven section headings.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers clear, welcoming, and open letterforms. Perfect for nutritional ingredient lists, recipe backstories, operating hours, and location specifics.
- **Price Displays**: Set in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` rather than serif to prioritize quick, scannable clarity alongside the Indian Rupee symbol (₹).
- **Botanical / Italic Notes**: Subheadings, preparation nuances (e.g., *steamed on natural teak leaves*), and scriptural blessings leverage Playfair Display Italic for gentle authenticity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces generous whitespace and relaxed vertical flow, rejecting dense corporate grids in favor of an unhurried morning dining feel.

- **Grid Architecture**: 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (`1200px` max-width constraint) transitioning to a 6-column grid on tablet and a single-column stacked format on mobile devices.
- **Section Rhythm**: Generous vertical section spacing (`space-xl` to `4rem`) provides an unhurried reading cadence, separating categories like Breakfast Tiffins, Signature Mudhas, and Accompaniments.
- **Content Alignment**: Editorial left-aligned headers matched with centered accent motifs (e.g., stylized millet stalk flourishes) above featured collections.
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Desktop**: Multi-card horizontal culinary spreads with generous photography margins.
  - **Tablet**: 2-column menu listings paired with sticky category side-tabs.
  - **Mobile**: Full-bleed swipeable trays and structured single-column vertical cards with thumb-friendly trigger tap targets (minimum 48px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on soft, sun-warmed ambient lighting combined with crisp tonal separation rather than sterile industrial dropshadows.

- **Warm Ghee Ambient Shadow**: Elevated elements utilize a dual-layer soft shadow tinted with deep forest and umber pigments:
  - Default Menu Cards: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 61, 46, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.04)`
  - Floating Overlays & WhatsApp Trigger: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 61, 46, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`
- **Filigree Borders**: Cards and panels employ delicate hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.25)`) that catch light like polished brass thali rims.
- **Tonal Layering**: Deep green surface bands (`#0F3D2E`) create stark theatrical breaks between the light millet-cream foundations (`#FAF6EC`), spotlighting catering announcements and founder stories.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, organic curvaceousness (Level 2: `0.5rem` / `8px` base up to `1rem` / `16px` for cards).

- **Menu & Location Cards**: Constructed with `rounded-lg` (16px), mirroring the soft contour of traditional South Indian serving trays.
- **Chips & Dietary Tags**: Full pill styling (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo rounded millet grains, mustard seeds, and steamed idlis.
- **Dividers & Flourishes**: Gentle organic curves, tapering gold leaf lines, and circular vignette frames for authentic food photography.

## Components

### Informational Menu Cards (No Carts)
- **Structure**: High-quality imagery container at the top or left, followed by the item title in `headline-sm` serif, dietary badge (100% Millet, Gluten-Free, Desi Ghee), brief culinary narrative in `body-sm`, and an integrated price callout.
- **Price Tag**: Highlighted prominently with a warm turmeric fill (`rgba(212, 160, 23, 0.12)`), deep forest green text, and crisp typography (`₹60/-`).
- **Border**: Enclosed by a 1px border (`#D4A017` at 25% opacity) that shifts to a solid accent gold (`#E5A93C`) on subtle hover.

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary CTA**: Deep forest green background (`#0F3D2E`), crisp cream label (`#FAF6EC`), and an accent gold border. Hover transitions to an amber glow (`#D4A017`).
- **Secondary CTA**: Transparent surface with a 1.5px gold border (`#D4A017`) and forest green text.
- **Action Triggers**: Direction links ("Get Directions") and dialer links ("Call to Order") feature clear icon pairings.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- **Form**: Compact pill capsules with 6px vertical and 12px horizontal padding.
- **Palette**: Pale banana-leaf green (`#E8F3EE`) background with `#0F3D2E` text for healthy offerings; light turmeric (`#FCF5DE`) with `#8F6B00` text for chef specials and ghee preparations.

### Review Badges & Social Proof
- **Visuals**: Framed cards containing gold-filled star clusters (`#E5A93C`), customer quotations in Playfair italic, and verified local patron tags.
- **Accent**: Small circular avatar with subtle gold rimming.

### Location Finder Cards
- **Elements**: Prominent landmark tags (e.g., "Kondapur, Hyderabad"), full street address in readable charcoal, operational morning & evening hours, and twin action buttons: "Navigate (Google Maps)" and "Call Kitchen".

### Floating WhatsApp Concierge Trigger
- **Position**: Fixed floating trigger in the bottom-right corner (`bottom: 2rem; right: 2rem; z-index: 50`).
- **Styling**: Forest green disc (`#0F3D2E`) with a glowing gold perimeter ring (`#D4A017`), housing the WhatsApp icon and a micro-pill badge reading *"Direct Orders & Catering"*.
