Crafted by Sowjanya & Manoj with Devotion and Care

When Sowjanya and Manoj started JSM, the vision was clear: build a breakfast sanctuary that treats every customer as extended family. Disheartened by the rise of overly refined maida, industrial food preservatives, and artificial soda used across breakfast hubs, they chose to revive ancestral kitchen principles.

Inspired by the guiding biblical principle “Love your neighbour as yourself”, every morning begins at 4:30 AM with cold stone-grinding, boiling fresh filtered water, and slicing fresh herbs. The promise is unwavering: nothing leaves the JSM counter that we wouldn’t serve to our own children.

Why Ragi & Millets Matter

Siridhanya grains were cultivated for over five thousand years in the drylands of the Deccan plateau. Here is why our bodies thrive on them.

Boasting 344mg of Calcium per 100g — thrice that of dairy milk. Packed with natural polyphenols, essential iron, and easily assimilated amino acids.

Our Five Kitchen Pillars

Transparency is not a marketing tactic; it is our holy standard. We display all ingredients and make every dish right in front of you.

01 100% Genuine Millets

Strictly pure ragi, jowar, foxtail, and urad dal. No white rice cut-ins.

02 Zero Soda & Colors

Naturally fermented overnight. Never puffed up with baking soda or stabilizers.

03 Stone-Ground Daily

Traditional slow wet-grinding keeps batters cool, preserving raw enzymes.

04 Pure Desi Cow Ghee

Drizzled generously over steaming tiffins for authentic South Indian fragrance.

05 Live Cooking Visual

Open hygienic stations. Watch your dosas roast and mudhas shape live.
