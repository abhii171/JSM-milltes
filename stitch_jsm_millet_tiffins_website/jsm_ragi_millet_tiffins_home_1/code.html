Reviving Ancient Grain Wellness for Modern Hyderabad

Founded with heartfelt intention by Sowjanya and Manoj, JSM Ragi & Millet Tiffins was born from a fundamental belief: everyday morning breakfasts should nourish the body without abandoning authentic Telugu culinary heritage.

In an era of hyper-processed flours and artificial soda leaveners, we grind freshly soaked finger millets (Ragi), Foxtail millets (Korralu), and sprouted legumes every single morning. We honor age-old fermentation cycles, pair our plates with slow-roasted flax and podi spices, and serve everything piping hot on traditional plantain leaves.
